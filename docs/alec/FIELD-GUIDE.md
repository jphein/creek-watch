# Creek Watch field guide (Sunday morning)

**App:** https://creekwatch.realm.watch. Open it on your phone and tap **Report**.
**Goal:** about 6 real reports with photos, from 2 or 3 spots, ideally on both creeks.
**Time:** about 2 hours, including driving. Be done by about 11:00 so JP can use the reports in the demo.

---

## Safety first

- **Bring someone with you.** If you can't, text JP where you're going and when you expect to be back.
- **Stay on the bank.** Don't wade in or climb down steep or slippery banks. Take every photo from dry ground.
- **Public places only.** Use parks, trails and the sidewalks on public bridges. If a sign says private property or no trespassing, skip that spot.
- **Watch your step.** Look out for poison oak, broken glass and needles. Wear closed shoes.
- **Don't touch anything strange:** dead animals, foam, oily sheen or odd-coloured water. Photograph it and report it, but keep your hands off it.
- **If something looks dangerous**, like a big spill or someone hurt, leave and call 911. The app is not for emergencies.
- **Don't photograph people's faces** or license plates. Point the camera at the water.

## At each spot: 5 photos, then 1 report

Take these photos with your normal camera first. They also give the demo video some footage.

1. **Wide shot:** the creek and the bank, so people can see the place.
2. **Close-up of the water:** its colour and how clear it is.
3. **Anything notable:** algae, trash, foam, a pipe or drain, wildlife.
4. **The flow:** a few seconds of video of the water moving.
5. **A landmark:** the bridge, trail sign or park sign (no people).

Then file **one report** in the app and attach the best photo.

---

## Filing a report (about 2 minutes)

1. Open the app and tap **Report**.
2. **Photo:** tap to take one or choose one. Allow location when the phone asks.
3. **Place:** check that the app picked the right creek and spot. Fix it if not.
4. **What you see:** tap the answers that match:
   - water colour (clear, cloudy, brown, green, other)
   - algae (none, some, lots)
   - trash (none, some, lots)
   - flow (dry, low, normal, high, flood)
   - smell (none, earthy, sewage, chemical, rotten, other)
   - dead fish (yes or no)
5. **Extras:** any wildlife you saw, and a short note.
6. **Name:** this is optional. Use your first name or leave it blank.
7. Tap **Send** and wait for the confirmation screen.

**Be honest.** If the creek looks fine, say so. Clean-water reports are real data too.

**Unsure?** Pick the closest answer and explain in the note, for example "slightly cloudy, maybe from yesterday's rain".
